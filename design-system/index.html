The command centre, one-handed

The QuoteMax mobile design system, tailored from quotemax.com.au for the app in a tradie's pocket. Same warm charcoal, same single yellow signal, same honest mono numbers, now with 48dp touch floors, both themes on every screen, and platform-native structure. The app consumes src/lib/theme.ts; this page is the human-readable source.

Colours

A warm charcoal monochrome lit by one hi-vis signal. Components consume semantic roles from theme.ts, never raw hex. Everything leans warm; a blue-black or cool grey anywhere is a defect.

The One Signal Rule

Yellow is the only accent. On any screen it marks the single thing that matters next. If two elements are yellow, one of them is wrong. Keep it under roughly 10% of the surface.

The Dark-on-Yellow Rule

Text and icons on a yellow fill are always dark charcoal. White on yellow sits near 1.4:1 and is forbidden.

The Warm-Not-Blue Rule

Canvas, borders and neutrals all lean warm, including themed platform components. Default Material blue-greys and iOS system greys get themed out.

Typography

Manrope, heavy and tight, against JetBrains Mono for anything instrumented: labels, refs, timestamps and every price. Sizes are dp and follow the OS font-size setting; only display caps its multiplier. Weights ship at 400 to 800 sans and 400 to 700 mono, nothing synthesised.

The All-Caps Display Rule

Display and headline are ALL CAPS Manrope 800, left-aligned, never centred, with at most one accent word. Line height stays at 1.05 or above on native; the web's 0.95 clips glyphs in React Native.

The Mono-Money Rule

Every price, ref and timestamp is JetBrains Mono with tabular figures, formatted by formatAud from integer cents. A price set in Manrope is a defect, not a style choice.

Logo

The two-tone M mark, cropped tight at 1.47:1. Body flips white on charcoal to charcoal on paper; the notch keeps the source gold #E3C13C in both themes. Ship it inline so it themes; size it by height and let width follow.

QuoteMax

Files: assets/logo/quotemax-mark-on-dark.svg · assets/logo/quotemax-mark-on-paper.svg. In-app the mark is an inline SVG component sized h-* w-auto; a square box letterboxes the landscape glyph. The wordmark stacks QUOTE over MAX at 0.82 leading and inherits the mark's body colour, so the lockup is always one colour and cannot drift. Clear space at least half the mark's height on all sides.

Depth & texture

Flat by intent. Structure is drawn with a 1px warm hairline and a lit top edge; cast shadows exist only for true overlays, tinted warm. Film grain earns its battery cost on hero surfaces only, and trade photography ships through the duotone pass so it reads as native to the palette.

Offline & loading

Offline · changes will syncApprovals queue and send when you're back in range.

Poor signal is the default scene. Skeletons are sunken blocks that reserve the widest expected figure, never A$0.00 and never a spinner. The offline banner is persistent, not a toast. An offline approval queues honestly: "QUEUED · SENDS WHEN BACK ONLINE", and the chip stays DRAFT until the backend confirms.

Motion

Fast, purposeful, and under 300ms in-app. Entries use a strong ease-out; nothing animates on a keyboard or system trigger, and Reduce Motion collapses everything to a crossfade or an instant cut.

Press feedback

Every pressable answers in 120ms: scale to 0.97 and one colour step, together. Ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1); ease-in is never used.

Rules

Screen transitions belong to the platform: push, sheet rise, predictive back, unrestyled. Chips and reveals take base 180. Sheets take slow 240. Anything a tradie triggers dozens of times a day does not animate at all.

Do and don't

The lines that keep the system honest. The full authority lives in DESIGN.md; these are the ones a reviewer checks first.

Do

  • Consume theme.ts roles. Both themes on every screen from the first commit.
  • One yellow signal per screen, dark ink on every yellow fill.
  • Draw structure: hairline plus lit edge, elevation 0 on resting surfaces.
  • Set money in mono with tabular figures, from integer cents via formatAud.
  • Keep targets at 48dp or better, rows and the primary CTA at 56dp. Test in sun, with gloves, in both themes.
  • Use stock platform navigation and controls, themed warm.
  • Run photography through the duotone pass before it ships in-app.

Don't

  • No navy and orange. The old Maintain identity is retired and must not creep back.
  • No pill buttons, glassmorphism, purple, gradients or emoji. Ever.
  • No white text on yellow, no yellow text on cream, no state colour as a large fill.
  • No shadows on resting surfaces, and no Material tonal tints cooling the charcoal.
  • No animation over 300ms in-app, none at all on high-frequency actions.
  • No invented prices. If the book has no price, the screen shows a skeleton or the A$99 site visit, never A$0.00.
  • No exclamation marks and no em-dashes in customer-visible copy. Full stop, comma, or middot.
